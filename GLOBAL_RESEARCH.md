## Purpose

This file is the reusable pre-writing evidence layer for the complete `advert.id` catalog. It consolidates advertising-media, campaign, site, graphic, material, structural, wind, fabrication, corrosion, electrical, digital-display, road/public-space, permit/tax, installation/K3, procurement, and maintenance research once, then maps it to every parent topic.

It is not a campaign plan, engineering design, permit/tax opinion, electrical design, lift plan, product specification, safe-work method, quotation, warranty, or permission to claim that an installed sign is safe, compliant, durable, legible, or effective. Writers must cite original sources and resolve the gates.

## How writers must use this file

1. Start with the topic row and open every mapped original source.
2. Separate advertising objective, message, media face, support structure, foundation/substrate, electrical/data system, site, installation work, and ongoing operation.
3. Treat standards abstracts as identity/scope evidence only; exact requirements require current full texts and the Indonesian project basis.
4. Never transfer a specimen, component, laboratory, or foreign-reference result to a complete installed sign.
5. Treat visibility, audience, traffic, impressions, conversion, price, tax, permit, and service-life figures as dated project evidence.
6. Use the exact current product TDS/SDS/manual, drawings, calculations, approvals, test records, and site conditions.
7. Preserve stop conditions for uncertain ownership/permission, unknown utilities/substrate, structural distress, unsafe access/lifting, live electricity, weather, public exposure, and unverified content.
8. Recheck volatile sources during outlining or six months after the date above, whichever comes first.

## Evidence scale

| Grade | Meaning | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Current Indonesian law, regulation record, or official government source | Establish status, jurisdiction, public scope, and high-level duties |
| B | Official standards-body abstract or current technical standard record | Establish terminology and method scope; full text and project adoption remain required |
| C | Government or recognized international technical guidance | Explain a bounded method with attribution; not Indonesian law |
| D | Manufacturer/product guidance | Explain only the documented product/configuration and current revision |
| Gate | Site-, design-, product-, test-, permit-, tax-, campaign-, or professional-specific evidence is unresolved | Do not publish definitive numbers, selections, safety, compliance, durability, or effectiveness claims |

### KR-02 — Advertising-media categories and claim responsibility

- **Sources:** [UU No. 8 Tahun 1999 consumer-protection PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Home/Download/33784/UU%20Nomor%208%20Tahun%201999.pdf), [PP No. 80 Tahun 2019 on electronic commerce](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/126143/pp-no-80).
- **Grade:** A.
- **Purpose:** Ground media taxonomy and responsible advertising claims without treating format choice as proof of result.
- **Summary:** Physical and electronic offers must present parties, objects, terms, and material information responsibly. Billboard, signboard, dimensional letters, light box, flexible light, LED display, vehicle graphic, wayfinding, and temporary banner are different systems.
- **Grounded facts:** A media name does not establish size, ownership, permitted location, structure, illumination, audience, availability, price, or performance.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `ADV-01`, `ADV-02`, `ADV-05`, `ADV-06`, `ADV-07`, `ADV-08`, `ADV-11`; define the complete system and claim owner.
- **Limits/recheck:** Content-specific advertising rules may also apply to food/drugs, finance, tobacco, politics, professions, children, or other regulated subjects.

### KR-03 — Campaign brief, measurement, and attribution

- **Sources:** [ISO 20252:2019 official overview](https://www.iso.org/news/ref2369.html), [ISO/TC 225 publication note](https://committee.iso.org/sites/tc225/home/news/content-left-area/news-and-updates/iso-202522019-market-opinion-and.html).
- **Grade:** B/C; a research-quality framework, not an outdoor-media effectiveness guarantee.
- **Purpose:** Ground objectives, sampling, measurement, reporting, and limits.
- **Summary:** ISO 20252 addresses planning, execution, supervision, and reporting for market/opinion/social research and data analytics; transparent definitions and traceability matter.
- **Grounded facts:** Reach, opportunity to see, traffic count, attention, recall, leads, visits, conversion, and sales are different measures. State population, period, method, sample, exclusions, attribution window, and uncertainty.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `ADV-02`, `ADV-03`, `ADV-06`, `ADV-07`, `ADV-08`, `ADV-14`; design a baseline and outcome log before installation.
- **Limits/recheck:** Obtain actual campaign, audience, site, traffic, channel, and sales evidence. Do not invent impressions, ROI, or causal lift.

### KR-05 — Artwork, colour, proof, and output-process control

- **Sources:** [ISO 12647-2:2013 official scope](https://www.iso.org/standard/57833.html), [ISO/TC 130 print-production guidance](https://committee.iso.org/files/live/sites/tc130/files/Resources/Guidelines%20for%20using%20print%20production%20standards%20v2%20Jan%202024.pdf).

### KR-08 — Wind action is a design input, not a sign-size rule

- **Sources:** [ISO 4354:2009 wind actions](https://www.iso.org/standard/38882.html), [current ISO 4354 revision record](https://www.iso.org/files/live/sites/isoorg/files/news/magazine/ISOupdate/EN/2026/iso_update_202601_en.pdf).
- **Grade:** B; foreign/international reference pending Indonesian project adoption.
- **Purpose:** Ground wind vocabulary and stop universal “safe size” advice.
- **Summary:** ISO 4354 addresses wind actions on structures, components, and appendages using defined methods; it is under revision in 2026.
- **Grounded facts:** Wind region/data, terrain/exposure, height, topography, directionality, shape, solidity, dynamic behavior, load combinations, importance/consequence, and full load path affect design.
- **Incorporation:** Use in `ADV-03`, `ADV-06`, `ADV-07`, `ADV-09`, `ADV-12`, `ADV-13`, `ADV-14`.
- **Limits/recheck:** A qualified engineer must use the current Indonesian project basis and site-specific inputs; the abstract supplies no calculation or acceptance.

### KR-09 — Welding, fabrication, and personnel qualification

### KR-17 — Inspection, defect diagnosis, maintenance, and lifecycle

- **Sources:** [ISO 12944-8:2017](https://www.iso.org/standard/64838.html), [Permen PUPR 10/2021](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/216875/permen-pupr-no-10-tahun-2021).
