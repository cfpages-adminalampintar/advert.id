# Topical Authority — advert.id

## Role and boundary

`advert.id` should become an Indonesian planning and delivery reference for physical advertising media and branded signage: translating a communication objective into a visible, readable, permitted, engineered, fabricated, installed, measurable, and maintainable asset. Its observed commercial routes support baliho, billboard, vehicle branding, raised letters, neon box, neon flex, name boards, running text, totem/pole signs, videotron, and permit/tax services.

Neutral content must keep campaign effectiveness separate from product sales and must not turn structural, wind, foundation, lighting, electrical, digital-control, traffic/public-realm, permit, tax, lifting, or work-at-height guidance into a project approval. Dimensions, capacities, foundations, fixings, circuits, luminance, operating hours, content controls, and installation methods require current local requirements, product/system evidence, project-specific design, and competent review.

No article is created by substituting a city, province, regency, or district. Related owned domains may cover the same entity independently; cannibalization control applies only inside `advert.id`.

## Internal-link architecture

- `/` links to core commercial media routes and a future neutral `panduan-media-advertising-fisik` hub (ADV-01-01).
- The hub routes readers through plan (ADV-02/03), choose/build (ADV-04–08), engineer/approve (ADV-09–11), deliver (ADV-12/13), and operate (ADV-14).
- Every guide links upward to its topic hub and to two to four true prerequisites/next steps. Product guides link to structural, electrical, permit and maintenance owners rather than repeating those sections.
- Diagnostic pages link to prevention, competent escalation, repair boundaries and replacement procurement.
- Commercial links appear only after a guide has qualified the medium and required evidence; informational pages do not disguise a sales CTA as a neutral conclusion.
- Breadcrumbs, HTML hubs, canonicals and sitemap membership must agree. Important guides should be reachable within three meaningful clicks.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current Indonesian and local primary sources at publication: applicable SNI/BSN, ministries/JDIH, road/building/advertising authorities, electrical and K3 requirements, product manuals/SDS, accredited tests and project specifications. Record edition, date, jurisdiction and scope.
- Never invent dimensions, wind speeds, member/foundation/fixing capacity, electrical load, luminance, pixel-pitch rule, permit/tax amount, price, lifespan, impressions, conversions, ROI, test result or project experience.
- Structural, wind, foundation, lifting and temporary-stability claims require project calculations and competent engineering review. Electrical, lighting, digital-control and weather-protection claims require system documents and competent review.
- Permit/tax articles describe a verification workflow, not a nationwide answer or a location-swapped sales page.
- Effectiveness content distinguishes exposure opportunity from verified viewing/action, documents baselines and confounders, and avoids causal claims without suitable measurement.
- Original photos require permission, date, asset identity and a statement of what the image cannot prove. Case studies need actual design, installation and outcome records.
- Every article has one primary job, explicit exclusions and a named owner for adjacent intent. Monitor GSC for same-domain overlap and consolidate when two URLs answer the same job.
